## Monty

*Newport Past: Garrison Town, Nursery of Vice*

Newport, in the 1850s. The Church in St. Thomas Square needs rebuilding. The less than salubrious Tontine Tap, just around the corner, and the nearby Paradise Row, with its promise of earthly delights, are renowned as "nurseries of vice" that tempt the young recruits stationed in the nearby army Barracks, before the troop ships take them on trips from which they may never return.

Into town comes a firebrand preacher, the Revd. Carus Wilson, on a mission to save the young men from themselves. A pamphlet is distributed widely around the town — "Tempters and Tempted" — calling out the lack of action by the magistrates. The Mayor's response provokes another, and a libel case is taken to defend the reputation of Francis Pittis, one of the town's most esteemed gentleman.

Years later, Carus Wilson's name will be lambasted in the press, and identified as the real person behind one of Charlotte Bronte's most despicable characters.

In this new historical storytelling show from Island based storyteller, Tony "Monty" Hirst, we meet some of the characters, and hear many of the tales, from a colourful period in Newport's past.

## Monty + The AnyWhens

*Faery Tale Blues*

A magical new set of stories and songs from Monty + The AnyWhens.

Following on from their sell-out show at Ventnor Fringe in 2025, folk duo Glenn Koppany and Sue Cain team up once more with traditional storyteller Tony "Monty" Hirst to bring dark versions of well-known fairy tales, original songs, and stories of the Fae Folk, to Ventnor Fringe for 2026.

Contemporary folk'n'blues tunes, playful lyrics and perfect harmonies, matched with tales that are familiar, but not, dating back to their origins, to a time when fairy stories weren't just for children, a time before they were sanitised, and sweetened, and made palatable for children's TV.

If you know your fairy tales, you'll know they often have a happy ending. But not always... And if you know your faery tales, you'll know you need to tread carefully...
