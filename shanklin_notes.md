JI.M * Shanklin Chine is a deep and winding chasm or cleft in the sea-cliff, penetrating into the land for a considerable extent, and clothed with various foliage and wild vegetation. The provincial term, "chine," is supposed to be derived from the common expression for a cleft in the ridge of an animal's back.—*Isle of Wight Guide.*
